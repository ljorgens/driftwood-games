Privacy Policy

Driftwood Games · Effective October 21, 2026

In short: This site has no accounts, ads, analytics or cookies, and stores nothing on your device. If you send a game idea or a mod, it reaches us by email through Formspree. If you choose to leave a tip, the payment is handled by Stripe.

Who we are

Driftwood Games is an independent developer of free browser games. In this policy, “we” means Driftwood Games. This policy covers this website. Each game is hosted on its own site with its own privacy policy, which applies while you play it.

What we do not collect

There is no sign-up, and there are no ads, analytics or cookies. This site does not store anything in your browser or track what you click. The only things it collects are what you choose to send: a game idea or a mod (see Ideas and mods below) or a tip (see Tips below).

Services that see basic connection data

Like every website, loading this one means your browser sends standard request information (such as your IP address, browser type and the page requested) to the service that delivers it. Render hosts the site, through its content delivery network provider, Cloudflare, and may keep standard server logs. See the Render privacy policy. The site’s fonts, videos and other files are all served from the same site, so no other company is contacted unless you send an idea or leave a tip. We do not use this information to identify you.

The site does no cross-site tracking and lets no third party do so, so there is nothing for “Do Not Track” browser signals to turn off.

Ideas and mods

If you use the “What should I build next?” form, we receive what you write, plus your email address if you choose to give one. If you send a mod, we also receive which game it is based on, its link, and the mod name and credit name if you fill them in. The form is delivered by Formspree, which emails it to us. On our current plan we can also see a copy in our Formspree account for 30 days. Formspree also receives standard connection data (such as your IP address and browser type), which it handles under the Formspree privacy policy, which also governs how long Formspree itself keeps it.

We use ideas to decide what to make next, and your email address only to reply to you. We never add you to a mailing list or share either one. We may mention an idea publicly (for example, “a player suggested this”), but never with your name or email. If we feature your mod, we show its link, name and description, and the credit name you gave (or no name if you left it empty), never your email. Please don’t include other personal details in the message. Messages are kept in our email for as long as they are useful, and you can ask us to delete yours, or take down a featured mod, at any time.

Tips

Tips are optional and unlock nothing. If you choose to leave one, you are taken to a checkout page run by Stripe, which collects and processes your payment details under the Stripe privacy policy. If you pay with Link, Apple Pay or your bank (such as Bancontact or EPS), that provider also processes your information under its own privacy policy.

Stripe shares details of the payment with us: the amount and date, your email address, the name and billing country or postal code you entered, and limited payment details (for cards, the brand, last four digits, expiry date and issuing country; for other methods, similar identifiers such as a bank name and last four digits). Stripe also shows us fraud-screening signals, such as the approximate location of the IP address used. We never see your full card or account number.

We use these details only to keep records of the tip, handle refunds or questions, prevent fraud, and meet tax and accounting obligations. We do not sell or share your personal information, use it for advertising, or add you to a mailing list. Payment records are kept for as long as tax law requires (generally up to seven years) and then deleted.

Links to other sites

This site links to our games, to GitHub (for source code) and to Stripe (for tips), and the ideas form sends to Formspree. Those sites have their own privacy policies, which apply once you visit them.

Children

This site does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. The form’s email and name fields are optional; children should leave them empty or ask a parent first. Tips should only be left by adults or with a parent’s permission. If a child tipped without permission, contact us within 60 days and we will refund it (see the terms).

Your choices

If you sent an idea or left a tip, you can ask what information we hold about it, or ask us to correct or delete it (apart from records tax law requires us to keep). See Contact below.

If you are in the EU or UK: connection data is processed for our legitimate interest in delivering the site, ideas and mods are processed for our legitimate interest in reading and replying to what you send (you can ask us to delete yours at any time), and tip records are processed to complete your payment and meet legal obligations. You may also complain to your local data protection authority. Our service providers may process data in the United States.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update it on this page and change the effective date above.

Contact

For refunds, privacy requests or anything else, email driftwoodgames@proton.me. If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you include in the message, and use them only to reply. Our email is hosted by Proton, under the Proton privacy policy.

See also the terms and tip refund policy.
